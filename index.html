About Me

Hi! My name is Eleanor and I am a fourth year undergraduate student at Northeastern University pursuing a Bachelor of Science degree in Data Science and Business Administration with a concentration in Supply Chain Management graduating in the Spring of 2028.

In the Fall semester of 2025, I was very fortunate to have the opportunity to work as an intern at Vicinity Energy with their Procurement team. Through this opportunity, I was able to gain valuable experience in supply chain management and a better understanding of the role and importance of procurement professionals through working and negotiating with preferred vendors, creating price comparisons for upcoming projects, and researching better pricing for frequently purchased items.

Through my coursework, I have gained experience working with, cleaning, analyzing, and creating visualizations of large data sets in Python. In addition to that, I have experience coding using object oriented programming, and creating and using machine learning models, as well as designing and creating databases in SQL. I aim to continue to refine my skills in Python and Data Science and to use these skills where applicable within supply chain management, such as to better store and visualize company data.

I am interested in gaining more professional experience and knowledge in other areas of supply chain management, such as transportation and logistics, as well as continuing to improve my coding abilities and data analytic skills. I am currently seeking co-op opportunities in supply chain management in the Boston area and am looking forward to connecting and learning about new opportunities!
